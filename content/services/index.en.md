### Sports Event Consulting
We support you from a focused advisory session to the full planning of a sports event. We define goals, operational structure, and practical actions so each decision is backed by strategy and clear execution.

**General consultation:** strategic meeting to answer questions about sports event management and provide actionable recommendations.

**Full event planning:** end-to-end event design and execution alongside your team, including goal setting, delivery planning, and a final evaluation with improvement opportunities.

### Administrative and Strategic Advisory
We conduct a sports management review to strengthen organizations and events. We can assess sponsorships, marketing, social media, administrative structure, workload distribution, budget use, facility conditions, and goal setting.

**Basic assessment:** review of information submitted electronically, evaluation report, and delivery through a virtual meeting.

**Comprehensive organizational assessment:** on-site information gathering, review of processes and facilities, and delivery of findings in an in-person meeting.

**Strategic plan:** roadmap with recommendations and concrete steps to improve the sports organization.

### Tournament Platform and Digitalization
We digitalize tournament operations to make them more practical and professional. The platform includes registration management, match schedules, statistics, standings, participant database, and real-time results.

It can also be integrated as a complement to other Atlas services so the full event operation stays in one digital environment.
